01 · Brand Overview

Meet NOORÉ

NOORÉ blends traditional oud, rose and amber notes with a minimalist, modern identity, sold direct-to-consumer through its website and social channels.

Mission

Make premium, long-lasting fragrance accessible to young urban professionals without losing cultural roots.

Target Audience

Men and women aged 20 to 40 in major Pakistani cities, active on Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp, who shop online.

Unique Value Proposition

Heritage-inspired scents, small-batch quality, elegant packaging and a try-before-you-commit discovery set.
